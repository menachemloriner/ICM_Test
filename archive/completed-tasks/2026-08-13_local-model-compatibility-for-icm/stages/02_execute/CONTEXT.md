# 02_execute — Execute

## Purpose
Perform the substantive work required by the task, regardless of whether that work
is research, analysis, writing, coding, editing, planning, or another bounded activity.

## Inputs
- Completed `TASK.md` and `pipeline.md`
- The current task's required source files and references
- The success criteria from `TASK.md`

## Process
1. Read the task-local status and the inputs required by this stage.
2. Perform the work in the task's `work/` directory or the explicitly named output
   location.
3. Keep material decisions, assumptions, and unresolved questions visible in the
   task record.
4. If a newly discovered requirement needs a distinct checkpoint or verification
   standard, pause and route it through a custom-stage decision rather than silently
   expanding the task.
5. Before finishing, check that the planned outputs exist and are ready for review.

## Outputs / exit criteria
- The planned work product exists.
- Important decisions and unresolved issues are recorded.
- The review stage has clear inputs and a clear verification target.
