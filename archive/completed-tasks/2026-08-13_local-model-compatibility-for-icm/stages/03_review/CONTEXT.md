# 03_review — Review

## Purpose
Check the work product against the task's success criteria, constraints, and evidence
requirements before delivery.

## Inputs
- Work product from `02_execute`
- `TASK.md` success criteria and required deliverables
- Any review-specific references named in `pipeline.md`

## Process
1. Check every success criterion and record pass, fail, or unresolved status.
2. Verify material claims, calculations, citations, behavior, or file outputs as
   appropriate to the task.
3. For each material verification, record the test or evidence used, the result, and
   the location of the supporting artifact. Do not mark a criterion verified based
   only on a general impression or checklist box.
4. Confirm the result stayed within scope and did not silently drop important
   requirements.
5. Identify revisions or user decisions needed before delivery. Ask the user when
   a material tradeoff is not obvious.
6. Apply or request revisions, then record the final review decision.

## Outputs / exit criteria
- Review findings are recorded.
- Material checks include evidence or a reproducible test, not only pass/fail labels.
- Required revisions are complete or explicitly accepted as unresolved.
- Every required deliverable is identified for the delivery stage.
